# 📖 Sunotal User Manual & Application Workflows

Complete step-by-step user manual and workflow guide for all 4 subdomain portals, role-based microservices, login procedures, vendor sourcing cycles, order tracking, dark store management, and delivery rider dispatches.

---

## 🔐 1. Access Credentials & Login Quick-Start

| Role | Subdomain URL | Login Email | Login Password | Access Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Consumer / Customer** | `sunotal.automateuniverse.space` | `user@sunotal.com` | `Devops@768` | Grocery browsing, checkout, live tracking, support tickets |
| **Farmer / Farm Vendor** | `vendor-sunotal.automateuniverse.space` | `vendor@sunotal.com` | `Devops@768` | Crop harvest quotation submissions, QC tracking, payout invoices |

## 🛒 2. Consumer Storefront User Manual (`sunotal.automateuniverse.space`)

### Step 1: Browse Grocery Catalog & Select Dark Store
1. Open `https://sunotal.automateuniverse.space`.
2. Click the **Express 10-15 Min Header** or location badge to select your local **Dark Store Fulfillment Hub** (e.g. *HSR Layout Dark Store #104*).
3. Use category pills (*Fresh Vegetables*, *Fruits*, *Dairy*, *Grains*, *Herbs & Spices*) or search bar to filter produce.

### Step 2: Add Items to Cart & Checkout
1. Click **+ ADD** on product cards to increment quantity using instant `- QTY +` counters.
2. Open the **Sticky Cart Drawer** from the top right to inspect subtotal, GST, delivery fee, and free shipping progress.
3. Click **Proceed to Checkout**. Select or add a delivery address.
4. Choose a payment method via the SOLID Payment Engine (*UPI QR*, *Card 3D-Secure OTP*, *NetBanking*, or *Cash on Delivery*).
5. Click **Pay & Place Order**.

### Step 3: Order History & Real-Time Live GPS Driver Tracking
1. Navigate to **My Orders** (`/orders`).
2. View your active orders with status badges (*Processing*, *Out for Delivery*, *Delivered*).
3. Click **Track Delivery** on any active order to open the interactive **Live GPS Driver Tracker**.
4. Inspect Leaflet vector map displaying:
   - 🏢 Dark Store Warehouse Hub
   - 📍 Customer Destination Address
   - 🛵 Live Moving Driver Icon
   - 🛣️ Turn-by-Turn Route Polyline
   - ⏱️ Dynamic ETA & Speed Banner (`ETA: 5 Mins`, `Speed: 34 km/h`)
   - 📞 Call Driver action button

### Step 4: Support Tickets & Produce Quality Rating
1. On delivered orders, click **Rate Order & Rider** to submit 1-5 star feedback.
2. If any produce item is damaged, click **Support Ticket** to submit a grievance (e.g. *Quality Issue* with preferred resolution *Full Refund*).

## 🌾 3. Farmer & Vendor Portal User Manual (`vendor-sunotal.automateuniverse.space`)

### Step 1: Vendor Sign In & Verification
1. Open `https://vendor-sunotal.automateuniverse.space`.
2. Login with `vendor@sunotal.com` / `Devops@768` (or click *Register as Farm Vendor* to submit a new farm application).
3. Inspect your **Verified Farm Vendor Profile Banner** (showing farm size, location, and approval status).

### Step 2: Submit Harvest Produce Supply Quotation
1. Click the **Submit Produce Quotation** tab.
2. Select Category (e.g. *Grains*, *Vegetables*, *Dairy*, *Fruits*).
3. Choose produce name from the auto-suggested catalog or type a custom crop name.
4. Enter supply quantity (e.g. `50`), unit (*Quintal*, *Kg*, *Liters*), and quoted unit price (₹/unit).
5. Select target **Dark Store Warehouse** for supply delivery.
6. Enter organic certification or irrigation notes.
7. Click **Submit Produce Supply Quotation**.

### Step 3: Track Quotations & Download Payout Invoices
1. Switch to **Harvest Supply Quotations** tab to monitor QC status (*Pending*, *Accepted*, *Rejected*).
2. Switch to **Direct Farmer Bank Payouts & Settlement Ledger** tab to view:
   - Total Settled Earnings (₹)
   - Pending QC Inspection Amount (₹)
   - Official GST HTML Payout Invoices generated by admin.
3. Click **Download HTML Invoice** on any settled row to view or print the official payout receipt document.

## 🛡️ 4. Admin Control Center User Manual (`admin-sunotal.automateuniverse.space`)

### Step 1: Admin Authentication & Dashboard
1. Open `https://admin-sunotal.automateuniverse.space`.
2. Login with `admin@sunotal.com` / `admin123`.
3. View high-level KPIs: Total Revenue, Active Orders, Registered Vendors, and Dark Stores.

### Step 2: Dark Store Warehouse Management
1. Navigate to **Dark Store Manager** (`/admin/warehouses`).
2. Click **Add New Dark Store** to register fulfillment centers (*Name*, *City*, *Address*).
3. Toggle Store Status (`Active` / `Inactive`) to manage stock allocation.

### Step 3: Catalog & Category Management
1. Navigate to **Categories** (`/admin/categories`) and **Product Definitions** (`/admin/product-definitions`).
2. Add new category icons or produce names to keep vendor submission forms aligned with platform demand.
3. Navigate to **Products Catalog** (`/admin/products`) to publish/unpublish items or edit pricing and discount badges.

### Step 4: Vendor Quotation QC Approvals & Invoice Generation
1. Navigate to **Vendor Quotations** (`/admin/quotations`).
2. Review pending farmer supply quotations.
3. Click **Accept Quotation**:
   - System automatically maps produce to catalog and seeds/allocates inventory stock to the selected Dark Store.
4. Click **Generate Invoice**:
   - System compiles GST invoice HTML, uploads to AWS S3 bucket (`jcs-raju-sunotal-final`), and links download URL.
5. Click **Update Payout Status** to mark payment status as `processing` or `paid`.

### Step 5: Financial Ledger & Telemetry Observability
1. Navigate to **Financial Ledger** (`/admin/ledger`):
   - Inspect total revenue, online vs UPI collections, PO receivables, completed settlements, and pending payouts.
2. Navigate to **Observability Telemetry** (`/admin/observability`):
   - View real-time microservices health status (Auth, Operations, Inventory, User, Delivery, Prometheus, Grafana).
   - Inspect live AWS Cloud spend breakdown (EKS, EC2, RDS, S3, Data Transfer).

## 🛵 5. Delivery Partner Application User Manual (`delivery-sunotal.automateuniverse.space`)

### Step 1: Rider Login & Duty Activation
1. Open `https://delivery-sunotal.automateuniverse.space`.
2. Login with `rider@sunotal.com` / `Devops@768`.
3. Toggle duty status switch to **ONLINE**.

### Step 2: Accept Hyperlocal Order Alerts
1. When an express grocery order is placed, an **Order Alert Modal** appears with a 30-second acceptance countdown timer.
2. View Customer Name, Pickup Dark Store, Delivery Address, Distance (km), and Earnings Pay (₹).
3. Click **ACCEPT DELIVERY ORDER**.

### Step 3: Turn-by-Turn Navigation & Delivery Stage Workflow
1. View the **Interactive Leaflet Route Map** displaying polyline navigation from Dark Store to Customer address.
2. Progress through order stages by clicking:
   - **Arrived at Warehouse Hub**
   - **Order Picked Up & Packed**
   - **Complete Delivery (Mark Delivered)**
3. Upon clicking **Complete Delivery**:
   - Toast notification confirms delivery.
   - Backend order status automatically updates to `delivered`.
   - Customer `/orders` page reactively updates in real-time.

### Step 4: Rider Earnings & Instant Day-Out UPI Payout
1. Inspect rider dashboard earnings stats: Completed Deliveries, Total Kms Run, Base Pay (₹30/order), Distance Pay (₹10/km), and Customer Tips.
2. Click **Request Day-Out Payout**. Enter UPI ID (e.g. `rider@upi`).
3. Click **Withdraw Earnings** to receive instant direct bank transfer.
